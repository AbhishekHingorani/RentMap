Gota is a developing area near North Ahmedabad in Gujarat District. This residential locality covers 6.78 sq. km with coordinates of 23.97154°N, 73.06986°E. Gita has total population of 79638 where male and female numbers are 41960 and 37678 respectively. People here experience extreme to moderate climate. The official languages of Gota are Gujrati and Hindi. Infrastructure and Details Gota came into limelight after the development of the Gandhinagar highway. As it was added to Ahmedabad Municipal Corporation, since then Gota falls under Ward no. 1 in New West Zone. Other than that, this place is well-connected with many neighbourhood localities like Chandlodiya (2.4km), Shayona City (3.5km), Vasant Nagar (1.9km) and Anand Vihar (94km). Also, the area has good connection via Jagatpur Road and Chandlodia Road. The highway connects with Gota and other parts of the city. The only airport in this place is Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel International Airport is about 15 km and the Indian Railway Network is almost 3.5 km away from this location. The closest Metro Station of Gota is Sabarmati (7km). Gota has numerous schools and colleges under its wing like Bhavin Vidya Vihar (17km), Nirma University (3.9km), Silver Oak Engineering College (2km), Aatman International School (2.7km) etc. And hospitals like Subham Hospital (1.7km), Medisure (4.8km), Kamdhenu Hospital (2.7km) etc. Around this area there are several malls located such as Elite Greens, Gota Circle, Melady Estate etc. Government offices such as Saffron Surveyors, Umiya Enterprise and Gujarat State Sales Tax office are all within 0.5 km distance. Nearest employment hubs are Adalaj, Gandhi Nagar, Thaltej etc. These places are about 3-5 km away from Gota. Property in Gota After the development of the highway, this area becomes one of the favourite spots for the Real Estate business. Gota offers a number of apartments, independent houses, penthouses, flats, villas, studio apartments etc. You can find minimum plot-size of 1200sq ft in this area.
