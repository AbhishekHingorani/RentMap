# Glossary

**Listing**: one rental home that someone is looking at. It is one row in the shared sheet. It may come from a site like 99acres, or a person adds it by hand.

**Status**: where a listing is in the hunt. One of: To call, Visit scheduled, Visited, Rejected. The sheet keeps it, but the app no longer shows it.

**Shortlisted**: a tick box on a listing, apart from its Status. A Visited listing can be shortlisted. It used to be a Status value.

**Configuration**: the size of the home by rooms. One of: 1RK, 1BHK, 2BHK, 3BHK, 4BHK+.

**Listed by**: who posted the listing. Owner or Dealer. A dealer listing usually means a brokerage fee.

**Rating**: a 1 to 5 score that people give a listing on one point: Safety, Water supply, Location, Condition (of the home itself), or Natural light. Each listing has one shared set.

**Our rating**: the average of Dimple's and Abhishek's ratings, each 0 to 10.

**Overall rating**: the average of a listing's 5 other ratings, each 1 to 5. The app shows it as "Others". When the user says "review", this is what they mean.

**Place**: a spot that matters to the people hunting, like an office, a school, or family. The map shows the distance from each listing to each place.

**Pin precision**: how sure a pin spot is. One of: building (the exact building), society (somewhere in the society), locality (somewhere in the area). A rough pin shows a dashed circle when picked.

**Duplicate listing**: a second listing for a home that is already in the sheet. A sure duplicate has the same listing page link. A possible duplicate has the same society and Configuration, and a person decides if it is the same home.
